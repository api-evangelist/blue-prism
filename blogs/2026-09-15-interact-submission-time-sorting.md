I have a process that will create Interact forms for colleagues to review data and provide direction to the robots. The issue is that when the forms are raised, the colleague portal is sorting the forms in newest to oldest date/time. While there is the option to manually sort them, if you go into a form or navigate away and back, the sorting is then reverted to newest.
