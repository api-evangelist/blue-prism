Hi everyone, Ever had work arrive just after a scheduled process ran and wondered why it has to wait for the next execution? We have a new course that explores a different approach. Automation Orchestrator uses conditions, triggers, and automation flows to coordinate work when it is actually needed, helping automation react to demand rather than the clock.
