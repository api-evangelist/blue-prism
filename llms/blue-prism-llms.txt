# SS&C Blue Prism

> SS&C Blue Prism helps complex enterprises make agentic AI operational through trusted, governed automation. Its platform connects people, AI agents, digital workers, APIs and business processes to help organizations design, orchestrate, manage and scale work across mission-critical and highly regulated environments.

SS&C Blue Prism provides enterprise agentic automation, robotic process automation, business process management, process orchestration and intelligent automation solutions. This file is the English-language content index for the global SS&C Blue Prism website and also provides access to the localized content indexes.
